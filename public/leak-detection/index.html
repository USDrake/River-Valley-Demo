Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if I have a hidden water leak?

Unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots, mold growth, or a drop in water pressure can indicate a hidden leak.

Can damaged copper pipes be repaired?

Pipe repair options depend on the location, age, and condition of the pipe. A professional inspection can determine the most suitable fix.
