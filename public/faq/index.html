Water Heater Services

Do you repair and install both traditional and tankless water heaters?

Yes. Our water heater services cover traditional tank-style systems and tankless water heaters, including troubleshooting, repairs, replacements, installation, and maintenance. We can help you review options based on your household's hot-water needs and the condition of your existing equipment.

How do I know whether my water heater needs repairs or replacement?

Common warning signs include inconsistent water temperature, running out of hot water, unusual noises, discolored water, or leaks around the unit. A repair may be appropriate for a smaller component issue, while a severely corroded, leaking, or aging unit may need replacement. An inspection can help determine the safest and most cost-effective option. If you notice a leak near electrical components, keep clear and seek professional help.

How long does a typical water heater last?

A traditional tank water heater commonly lasts about 10–15 years, while many tankless systems can last around 20 years with proper maintenance. Actual lifespan depends on water quality, usage, installation, and maintenance. If your water heater is older or showing signs of trouble, an inspection can help you plan for repair or replacement.

Plumbing Repairs & Maintenance

What should I do if I discover a water leak in my home?

If it's safe to do so, shut off the water supply to the affected fixture or your home's main water valve. Move valuables away from the water and avoid wet areas where electricity may be present. Then call a plumber to locate the source and assess the repair. A significant or uncontrollable leak should be treated as an urgent plumbing issue.

How can I tell if I have a hidden plumbing leak?

Warning signs can include an unexpected increase in your water bill, damp walls or flooring, musty odors, stains, reduced water pressure, or the sound of water running when no fixtures are in use. You can also check whether your water meter changes when all water-using fixtures and appliances are off. Professional leak detection can help locate the cause without unnecessary guesswork.

What are the signs that my pipes need to be repaired or replaced?

Watch for recurring leaks, visible rust or corrosion, damp spots, discolored water, changes in water pressure, or frequent plumbing problems in the same area. Some problems can be repaired locally, while widespread corrosion or repeated failures may make pipe replacement the better long-term option. A plumbing inspection can help determine the condition of your pipes.

Why do my drains keep clogging, and when should I call a plumber?

Recurring clogs can be caused by grease, hair, soap residue, food debris, or buildup deeper in the drain line. If a drain keeps backing up, multiple fixtures are affected, or you notice gurgling, sewage odors, or wastewater coming up through a fixture, it's time to call a plumber. Professional drain cleaning can address the blockage and help identify whether a larger pipe issue is involved. Avoid mixing chemical drain cleaners.

Can you help with low water pressure?

Yes. Low water pressure can result from leaks, mineral buildup, partially closed valves, fixture problems, or issues with the water supply. If the pressure drops suddenly or affects several fixtures, contact us so we can help identify the cause and discuss an appropriate repair. If the problem affects your whole neighborhood, your water utility may also be able to help.
